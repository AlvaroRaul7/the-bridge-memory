# Acme Corp — Contract Summary

*Contract ID: ACME-2025-003. Effective 2025-03-15. Owned by Legal (Dana Whitfield).*

## Term and value

- **Term:** 3 years, 2025-03-15 through 2028-03-14.
- **ARR:** $840,000.
- **Seats:** 500 (Platform tier) + Analytics module.
- **Payment terms:** Annual, in advance, Net 45.
- **Auto-renewal:** Yes, 12-month rolling unless either party gives 90 days written notice.

## Commercial terms

- **Uplift cap:** Annual price increase capped at **4%** at each anniversary.
- **Seat true-up:** Acme may add seats mid-term at the contracted per-seat rate ($1,680/seat/yr). Seats cannot be reduced mid-term.
- **Discount:** 18% off list, contingent on the 3-year commitment.

## Service levels

- **Uptime SLA:** 99.9% monthly.
- **Service credits:** 10% of monthly fee per 0.1% below SLA, capped at 30% of monthly fee.
- **Support response:** P1 within 1 hour, P2 within 4 business hours, P3 within 2 business days.

## Notable clauses

- **Clause 7.3 — Termination for convenience:** Not available to Acme. Termination for cause only (30-day cure period).
- **Clause 11.2 — Data residency:** All Acme data processed in EU regions only.
- **Clause 14.1 — Benchmarking:** Acme may request a market-rate benchmark once per 12 months; no automatic price adjustment attaches to it.

## Renewal posture (as of Jan 2026)

Renewal is not due until 2028. Any early renewal conversation would be a *strategic* renegotiation, not a required one. Our leverage is the 3-year term; their leverage is the seat under-utilisation.
