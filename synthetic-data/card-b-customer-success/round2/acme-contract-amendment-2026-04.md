# Acme Corp — Contract Amendment No. 1

*Amendment ID: ACME-2025-003-A1. Executed 2026-04-08. Amends ACME-2025-003.*

## Summary

Acme exercised its Clause 14.1 benchmarking right in Q1 2026 and used the result to
negotiate this amendment in exchange for extending the term by 12 months.

## What changed

| Term | Was (2025-03-15) | Is now (2026-04-08) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Term end | 2028-03-14 | **2029-03-14** (extended 12 months) |
| Seats | 500, no mid-term reduction | **400**, with a one-time reduction right exercised at signing |
| ARR | $840,000 | **$688,000** |
| Uplift cap | 4% annually | **2.5%** annually |
| Termination for convenience | Not available | **Available from 2027-04-01 with 120 days notice** |
| Support response P2 | 4 business hours | **2 business hours** |
| Service credit cap | 30% of monthly fee | **50% of monthly fee** |

## Unchanged

- Clause 11.2 data residency (EU only) — unchanged.
- Per-seat true-up rate — unchanged at $1,680/seat/yr.
- Auto-renewal mechanics — unchanged.

## Commercial read

We traded **$152k of ARR and a termination-for-convenience right** for 12 months of term
and the removal of the seat under-utilisation argument. The tighter P2 SLA and the higher
credit cap mean the ACME-4288 class of problem is now materially more expensive for us.

**Important:** any renewal proposal built on the January 2026 contract summary is wrong.
The $840k / 500-seat / 4%-uplift / no-termination picture no longer exists.
