# Acme Corp — Leadership Change

*Source: Acme press release + Devon Ellis briefing call, 2026-05-11.*

## What changed

**Sarah Chen has left the CTO role.** Effective 2026-04-30 she moved to a newly created
**Chief Strategy Officer** position at Acme. She no longer owns the technology budget and
is not a decision-maker on vendor renewals.

**Devon Ellis has been promoted to CTO**, effective 2026-05-01. Devon was previously
Director of Platform Engineering and is our day-to-day technical owner.

## Why this matters for us

- Devon is the person who has been most vocal about our support response times and about
  ticket ACME-4288. Our champion is gone and the seat has been taken by our biggest
  internal critic.
- Sarah signed the 2025 renewal and was sympathetic to our pricing. Devon was not part of
  those commercial conversations.
- Marta Ruiz (Procurement) now reports into Devon's org rather than into Finance. That
  consolidates the pricing pressure and the technical dissatisfaction under one leader.

## Also changed

- **Hiro Nakamura left Acme** in March 2026 for a competitor. We lost our strongest
  bottom-up advocate. No replacement identified for the internal user community he ran.
- Acme has stood up a formal **Vendor Review Board** that meets quarterly. Our next review
  slot is 2026-09.
