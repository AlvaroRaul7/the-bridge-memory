# Project Lighthouse — FY2025 Financial Restatement

*Data-room tranche 3, delivered 2026-06-12. Source: draft audit opinion, Karsten & Vale LLP.*

## The auditors restated FY2025 revenue

The FY2025 figures we assessed in tranche 1 were **unaudited management accounts**. The
draft audit restates them:

| Metric | Tranche 1 (unaudited) | Tranche 3 (restated, audited) | Delta |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Revenue | $62.4M | **$54.9M** | **–$7.5M (–12%)** |
| YoY growth | 39% | **23%** | –16 pts |
| Gross margin | 76% | **71%** | –5 pts |
| EBITDA | $7.8M | **$0.4M** | **–$7.4M (–95%)** |
| Net revenue retention | 118% | **104%** | –14 pts |

## Why

1. **$5.1M of multi-year professional-services revenue was recognised upfront** rather than
   over the delivery period. ASC 606 treatment corrected on audit.
2. **$1.7M of "usage overage" revenue was billed but never collected** from two customers
   now in dispute. Reversed to a bad-debt provision.
3. **$0.7M of reseller revenue was recognised gross rather than net.**

## Management's position

Management characterises this as "a technical accounting adjustment, not a business change."
CFO Bill Doherty ran both the FY2025 close and this restatement.

## Analyst note

Point 1 is a policy error. Point 2 is a **collections and disclosure** problem — the $1.7M
was described to us in tranche 1 as "usage overage" revenue with no mention of dispute.
The tranche-1 representation that there was **no pending or threatened litigation** was
made on 2026-02-06; the disputes underlying point 2 predate that date.

## Revised valuation arithmetic

At the unchanged $520M ask, the target now trades at **9.5x revenue** and **1,300x EBITDA**
— outside the 6.5x–9.0x comparable range on revenue and meaningless on EBITDA.
