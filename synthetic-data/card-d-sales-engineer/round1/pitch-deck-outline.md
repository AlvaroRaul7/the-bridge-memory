## Positioning strategy

**Lead with latency.** It is our clearest technical win over Northwind (they publish 180ms
p99) and it is the thing Stefan will test hardest. Winning slide 4 wins Stefan; winning
Stefan wins the deal.

**Secondary differentiator:** deployment flexibility. Northwind is SaaS-only and cannot
meet requirement 2 at all.

**Concede early:** UI polish. Northwind's UI is better. Acknowledge it on slide 3 rather
than letting Stefan raise it.
